Events

Where we show up. What we're building toward.

Programs, forums, and events designed to make technical careers visible and accessible—for youth, veterans, and the broader community in West Palm Beach.

Youth Programs

Discovery for young people.

Hands-on workshops, camps, and mentorship sessions that introduce young people ages 8–18 to technical careers, research fields, and the people who work in them. Programs cover career exploration, portfolio development, and science fair mentorship.

Youth

Technical Discovery Workshops

Intro to what technical careers actually look like day to day—covering research, engineering, and defense fields. No experience needed.

Youth

Summer Discovery Camp

A two-week youth technical discovery program. Parents and guardians welcome at the kickoff session.

Youth

Science Fair Mentorship

Year-round mentorship program pairing students ages 12–18 with working researchers and engineers. Parents welcome.

Community Engagement

Open to everyone.

Forums, workshops, and dialogues designed to make research and technical careers visible and accessible to adults, families, and the broader community—no prior background required.

Community

Researcher Dialogues

Open Q&A with working researchers on what defense and technical research actually looks like and how communities can participate.

Community

Career Awareness Workshops

Hands-on workshops introducing technical career paths, digital tools, and local opportunities—in plain language.

Community

Monthly Researcher Forum

Recurring open Q&A with engineers and researchers. All questions welcome—career, project, or pure curiosity.

Veteran Transition

From service to technical career.

Intake sessions, MOS translation workshops, and employer pipeline events designed for veterans transitioning to civilian careers. The goal is direct connection to employers—not just preparation.

Veterans

Veteran Pathway Intake

Intake walkthrough, MOS translation examples, and overview of our employer pipeline. Bring your DD-214.

Veterans

Defense & Technical Careers Workshop

Exploring defense and technical career pathways. Translation of military experience to civilian technical roles.

Veterans

Employer Showcase

Connect directly with defense contractors and technical employers actively hiring veterans. Resume reviews and on-site conversations.
